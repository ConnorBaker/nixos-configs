Our applicability:

Regular nixpkgs packages remain input-addressed; feature enablement alone does not turn them into floating CA packages. Thus benefits only workloads actually using __contentAddressed or dynamic derivations, no expected general evaluation speedup or ordinary compilation throughput improvement.
ExternalDerivationBuilder inherits UnixDerivationBuilderImpl and sends its scratchOutputs in build JSON to tribuchet, so floating CA scratch fix affects both local builds and Tribuchet external builds. Registration fix applies to whichever daemon/store records CA output, including gRPC remote builders; all participating build/registration daemons should run matching patched Nix. Tarmac plugin is evaluation store, these are ordinary build-store/goal fixes; no direct plugin behavior change or conflict identified.
