Sergei Zimmerman's draft#16427
https://github.com/NixOS/nix/pull/16427
Still open/draft. Disables redundant Git object hash verification on reads,
uses patched libgit22.0RC with zlib-ng, and disables expensive delta search
for tarball-cache packs, including libgit2 configuration fixes. Author reports
~3x less CPU time and wall time5.55s->3.04s on his cold-cache benchmark; no
measurement on our hosts. Not included in current upstream pin, four core
patches, or plugin source/package overrides. Our libgit2 is1.9.7, without
these patches, linked to zlib1.3.2 rather than zlib-ng.

Historical correction: the earlier Determinate3.23.1 pin ea7e9be2050705f62414cd8657bb656b52477d40
DOES include this work through merged Determinate PR627. Merge8a5cc816 is
an ancestor (326commits ahead, zero behind). Historical git-utils.cc contains
strict-hash disabling and dontFindDeltas/pack.window=0. Switching to upstream
Nix dropped those source changes; Tarmac did not import them indirectly.
https://github.com/DeterminateSystems/nix-src/pull/627

Tarmac replaces the tarball InputScheme with its own libarchive ingestion,
BLAKE3/LZ4/LMDB pack store, avoiding that Git-cache work for ordinary tarball
inputs. It does NOT replace github/gitlab/sourcehut/git InputSchemes. Our
pinned github.cc directly obtains getTarballWriterPool/getTarballCache and
unpacks archives into the Git cache, bypassing the tarball InputScheme registry.
Thus the draft remains relevant to our predominantly github: flake inputs,
even with Tarmac, but is not a Tarmac compatibility prerequisite.
